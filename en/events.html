More than entertainment

A moment that brings people together

Tarot entertainment does not distract your guests — it gathers them. Each person waits their turn, compares, tells the story. Conversations start on their own, including between people who would never otherwise have spoken.

I work with the Tarot de Marseille under one rule: kindness. No blunt predictions, no unsettling theatrics. An honest, personal moment, still worth retelling the next day.

Your questions

What organisers ask me

How long per guest?

Five to ten minutes in the Discovery format, longer in the Evening format. The pace is set by the number of guests and the length of your event: we agree on it beforehand, so that nobody is left out.

Does it work for corporate events?

It does, and it is a setting I particularly enjoy. The approach is deliberately light: we talk about intuition, momentum, direction — never blunt predictions. Your colleagues leave with a good moment, not with a worry.

What if our evening has a theme?

Gatsby, Halloween, bohemian, cabaret, Venetian… A matching outfit is an option at CHF 50. Tell me the world of your event and I will propose something. The décor itself stays on your side.

Fill in the quote form and I reply within 24 hours with a tailored proposal. The date is held once the quote is signed.
