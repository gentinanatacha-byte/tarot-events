My rule

I work with the Tarot de Marseille under a single rule: to shed light without confining, to guide without ever imposing.

You leave with clarity about a situation, not with a verdict. The cards show a dynamic and a set of possibilities; what you do with them is entirely yours.

What I work with

Questions of the heart, periods of transition, professional choices, family ties. And sometimes, simply, the need to see more clearly without yet knowing about what.
