What we work on

The twenty-two major arcana, one by one, and what they actually say — far beyond the keywords you find everywhere.

Then the spreads: how to ask a question that means something, how to read a whole rather than a string of isolated cards, and how to stop when you do not know.

I also work with you on what frightens people most at the start: saying things out loud. Finding the right words, staying generous, and never confining the person in front of you.
