My background

A story of transmission

I was born in Geneva. Very young, I discovered readings alongside my grandmother, a medium well known in this city, who passed on to me both a way of listening and a love of the cards.

Over the years, that transmission became a practice of its own. The Tarot de Marseille, my intuitive listening and the reading of energies came together in a single approach: to shed light without confining, to guide without ever imposing.

For more than ten years I have worked with questions of the heart, periods of transition and professional choices — with clarity, honesty and kindness. More than five hundred readings later, I am back working in the Lake Geneva region, where it all began.

Event work

I also work in settings very different from the intimacy of a private reading: private parties, corporate events, reception venues.

The same requirement applies everywhere — entertainment that is elegant, discreet and tailored, adapting to the audience and the venue rather than the other way round.

My method

The Tarot de Marseille, and nothing else

This is the deck I use, and the only one. Its graphic simplicity, its refusal of sentimentality and its symbolic rigour make it a demanding reading tool — and an honest one.
